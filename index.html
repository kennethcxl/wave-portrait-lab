About this experiment ↗

Inspired by the speaker portraits on Cursor Compile New York. The sample is an original AI-generated portrait. Choose a close-up with clear lighting for the most recognizable result. Images are processed locally and are never uploaded.

The original client-side sine-wave shader and luminance preprocessing are adapted from Cursor’s deployed implementation, inspected on 19 October 2026. Brightness controls wave amplitude and frequency; staggered rows create the portrait. This independent study adds editing, comparison, and export. The adapted rendering code retains its original rights.
